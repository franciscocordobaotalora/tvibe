# Tell it the goal

Copy this into your LLM. Replace anything in <angle brackets>.

```
I have a Samsung Smart TV, model <YOUR MODEL, for example UE43CU7172>. I want a custom home dashboard app for it: a clock, a greeting, and big cards for <YOUR 5 APPS>. Tell me what I need, then guide me one step at a time. Ask me for anything only I can see on the TV.
```
